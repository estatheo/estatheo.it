PROMENAUT

Aug 2026 to now · Senior Platform Engineer · London

The line on my LinkedIn is "Building Agentic Workforce": agents that do real work inside a company.

The part I spend most thought on is governance. An agent that acts for a company has to work under written rules, stop and ask a person when a rule says so, and leave an audit trail of what it did.

It is the same habit as the ledger at Playter: nothing happens without a record.

I run the same idea at home, as a graph of coding agents: one plans the work, others do it, one watches them and one merges what passes. Most of the projects were built that way.
